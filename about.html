Sunrisers Hyderabad

The Sunrisers Hyderabad is a franchise cricket team based in Hyderabad, Telangana, India...

They compete in the Indian Premier League (IPL) and have won the tournament twice.

Known for their strong batting attack and consistent performances, they have a passionate fan base.

Key players include David Warner, Kane Williamson, and Bhuvneshwar Kumar.

The team is owned by Kalanithi Maran of the Sun TV Network and plays its home matches at the Rajiv Gandhi International Cricket Stadium.
